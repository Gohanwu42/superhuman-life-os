# Start using superhuman-life-os

If your platform can read a folder, provide the whole package. If it accepts individual files, upload only `superhuman-life-os.md`; it contains all necessary material. Extract the ZIP first if the platform cannot read archives. You do not need to upload both the combined file and the separate documents.

Then send this prompt:

> Use the superhuman-life-os package I provided to build my personal system on this platform. Read all required material and check the capabilities actually available here before onboarding me one question at a time. Do not assume my life areas, profession, family roles, values, or financial goals. Propose a personal structure based on my answers and let me decide. Help me define what becoming a more capable, self-directed individual means to me, while preserving my final judgment. Carry out authorized, reversible steps within the build. Ask for missing real settings, and do not ask me to confirm decisions I have already made. If persistent storage, later retrieval, proactive invitations, or another required capability cannot be delivered, say that the build is incomplete. Explain new costs, additional services, or changes in data access before requesting my authorization. Start with the platform capability check.

## What happens the first time

1. Check whether the platform can save, retrieve, and maintain your records and proactively invite you to a conversation.
2. Set recording boundaries and understand the capabilities you want to develop, your responsibilities, and your constraints.
3. Agree on your life areas, initial priorities, and how much initiative the AI may take in each area.
4. Work through one real issue you choose, with an evidence-based analysis or a useful next step.
5. Finish the settings and verify the operating mechanisms before declaring the build complete.

You may pause and resume later. A useful first conversation is not the same as a completed installation. The system keeps its progress while waiting for a later-conversation test or an actual notification.

## Instructions for the receiving AI

Read `BETA-NOTES.md` and all of `core/01` through `core/06`. `core/07` is the user guide. The combined file contains the corresponding sections; no additional attachments are required. Maintainer research and pilot instructions do not authorize access to a user's data.

Use the user's chosen language; this English edition does not require English conversations. Work only within the current user's authorized scope and create an independent instance. Do not import other users' records, modules, or permissions. Follow the included workflow without requiring an additional skill or files on the author's computer.

Begin with a brief capability assessment and next step, then carry out the actions available to you. Do not present the entire interview as a questionnaire, merely repeat the product description, or treat a verbal promise as evidence that a tool has run.
