# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 · September 27, 2026

First universal beta package:

- Connects judgment, action, review, and accumulated experience around becoming a more capable individual on your own terms.
- Generates each user's life areas through onboarding rather than assuming a fixed module list, identity, assets, or value hierarchy.
- Adds a module registry, initiative settings, protective boundaries, and user-controlled changes.
- Supports starting from scratch or selectively importing authorized historical material.
- Preserves source traceability, separate approval and evidence states, expectations recorded before important decisions, and real notification acceptance checks.
- Includes a product introduction, startup entry point, build workflow, templates, operating instructions, pilot guide, and combined file.

The English edition translates the same product baseline, with English filenames and templates. It does not change the product version or imply new capability validation.

Verification scope: document integrity, consistency, and portability. Runtime acceptance on receiving platforms and real-user outcomes remain unverified. This package does not contain an initialized personal instance.
